Straw Flute — Cover the Holes

Sounding length: 20.0 cm (all holes covered) | Frequency ≈ 429 Hz | Note ≈ A4
Holes (checked = covered / closed)
The pitch is set by the hole closest to the mouth that's uncovered — holes further down don't matter once air can escape early. The holes are placed to play a major scale: uncover them one at a time from the far end (6, then 5, …). You can also tap a hole directly on the straw.

A drinking straw becomes a flute with six finger holes. An uncovered hole lets the air escape, so the air column effectively ends there: the sounding length is set by the uncovered hole closest to the mouth, and holes further down no longer matter. A shorter column plays a higher note:
