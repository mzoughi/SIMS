4) Send to the answer boxes

This tool measures positions on a photograph or a scanned graph: load an image, set the scale by clicking two points of known value, then click to record positions (x only, y only, or both). The table, the spacing between successive positions and the buttons that send values to the answer boxes update as you go.

The scale converts pixels to real units. For two reference points at pixels `p_1` and `p_2` with known values `X_1` and `X_2`,

`X = X_1 + (p - p_1)(X_2 - X_1)/(p_2 - p_1)`.

The spacing tool finds the average distance between equally spaced features, such as interference fringes or tick marks, with its uncertainty. Zoom and the magnifier help place each click precisely.
