Motion Summary

A glider moves on a frictionless horizontal track, and the graphs record its position and its velocity against time. Drag the glider, or play an example, and watch how its motion appears on each graph.

The slope of the position-time graph is the velocity: a flat line means the glider is at rest, a steeper line a faster motion, and a downward slope a motion in the negative direction. The slope of the velocity-time graph is the acceleration: a flat velocity line means a constant velocity, and a sloped one a speeding up or slowing down.
