This simulation shows a charge `q` moving from a point A to a point B in a uniform electric field `vec E`. The charge, the strength and direction of the field, and the positions of A and B can be changed.

The electric force on the charge is `vec F = q vec E`, constant everywhere. Over the displacement `Delta vec r` from A to B, the work done by the field is

`W_E = q vec E * Delta vec r`,

which depends only on the end points, not on the path. The change in electric potential energy and in electric potential are then

`Delta U = -W_E`,   `Delta V = (Delta U)/q = -vec E * Delta vec r`.

Moving along the field lowers the potential; moving perpendicular to the field does not change it. A positive charge moving along the field loses potential energy, a negative one gains it.

This simulation is useful for studying work, electric potential energy and electric potential in a uniform field.
