Results

A velocity-time graph made of constant "steps" describes a cart on a frictionless track. Read the velocity at each listed time, then predict the position-time and acceleration-time graphs. The change in position over an interval is the area under the velocity graph, and the acceleration is its slope:

`Delta x = int v dt`  (area),   `a = (Delta v)/(Delta t)`  (slope).

Two draggable markers on the velocity graph shade and compute the area between them, to check (not replace) your own calculation.

The cart's true motion stays hidden: only the graph is real data. After you submit, the sim grades your entries, graphs your values against the true ones, and plays the motion back on the 1.2 m track so you can check your work.
