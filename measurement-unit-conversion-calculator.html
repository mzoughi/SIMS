To convert a quantity, choose what it is (a length, a mass, a speed, a temperature…), type the value, and choose the units to convert from and to. The result shows the value in the new units, and Show the math explains each step.

Most conversions multiply by a conversion factor: 1 km = 1000 m, so 2.5 km × (1000 m / 1 km) = 2500 m. The factor equals 1, so the quantity itself does not change, only the units it is expressed in. Temperatures are different: the Celsius and Fahrenheit scales have different zeros, so a conversion also adds or subtracts an offset (°F = 1.8 × °C + 32).

The result keeps the number of significant figures of the value entered, since a conversion cannot make a measurement more precise.
