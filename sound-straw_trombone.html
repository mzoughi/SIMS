A second, thinner straw slides in and out of the reed straw like a trombone slide, continuously changing the total tube length instead of cutting it.

A second, thinner straw slides in and out of the reed straw like a trombone slide. Instead of cutting the straw, the slide changes the length of the air column continuously, so the note glides up and down. The reed end acts as a closed end and the far end is open:

`f = v/(4L)`,

so pulling the slide out lowers the note, and pushing it in raises it.
