Ohm's Law: Carbon Resistor & Incandescent Lamp

Pick the carbon resistor or the incandescent light bulb below. For the carbon resistor, set its resistance value and its starting (ambient) temperature. For the light bulb, set its rated wattage -- which determines its cold resistance -- and its starting (ambient) temperature. Drive the circuit with either the LVPS (voltage) or Current slider (moving one updates the other to match). Click "Collect Data Point" to log the ammeter/voltmeter reading -- with realistic random measurement error -- onto the graph. For the carbon resistor, "Show Linear Fit" draws a best-fit line through your collected points; its slope is the resistance (read it from the graph, not from any other readout). For either device -- especially the light bulb, or the resistor pushed hot enough to show slight curvature -- drag the "Explore Slope At" slider to read the local (dynamic) slope anywhere along the theoretical curve.
