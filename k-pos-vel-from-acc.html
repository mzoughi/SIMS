Your Task

The acceleration-time graph below shows a cart's motion on a 1.2 m track over 10 s (the equation behind it is hidden -- only the step shape is real data). You're also given the cart's starting position and velocity. Read the cart's acceleration at each listed time, then use your own readings to calculate its velocity (area under a-t = Δv) and then its position (area under v-t = Δx). Enter everything in the tables, then submit to see how you did -- and to watch the real motion play out.

Results

An acceleration-time graph made of constant "steps" describes a cart on a frictionless track, with its starting velocity and position given. Read the acceleration at each listed time, then predict the velocity-time graph and, from it, the position-time graph: each graph is built from the area under the one before.

`Delta v = int a dt`,   `Delta x = int v dt`.

Two draggable markers on the acceleration graph shade and compute the area (the change in velocity) between them, to check your calculation.

The cart's true motion stays hidden: only the graph is real data. After you submit, the sim grades your entries, graphs your values against the true ones, and plays the motion back on the 1.2 m track so you can check your work.
