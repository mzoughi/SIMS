Add / Remove a Meter

Choose a meter (or "Remove Device"), then pick a zone from the dropdown -- or click zone 1, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, or 11 directly on the diagram.

Connect / Disconnect a Wire

You can also click any two connection points on the diagram, one after another, to connect or disconnect them. Hover over a point first to see its name in the hint line above. On the diagram, small gray dots are fixed board pads, while a placed meter's own two terminals are the larger blue-ringed dots right next to its body -- click those to wire the meter itself, not the pad behind it. If you'd rather not aim on the canvas, the Point A / Point B dropdowns above name every point exactly and never miss.
