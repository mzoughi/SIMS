Up to five point sources make circular waves on the water of a ripple tank, each with its own frequency, amplitude and position. Where waves from two sources overlap they interfere: crest on crest builds a bigger wave, crest on trough cancels it. For two sources in phase with the same frequency, at a point whose distances to them differ by `Delta d`,

constructive: `Delta d = m lambda`,   destructive: `Delta d = (m + 1/2) lambda`,   `lambda = v/f`.

The buoy records the water level at one point over time, and the intensity along the dashed line shows the bright and dark bands, the same pattern as light through a double slit. Drag the numbered sources and the buoy on the tank.
