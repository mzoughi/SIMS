Standing sound waves in a tube that is open at both ends, closed at both ends, or open at one end and closed at the other. The molecules of air move back and forth along the tube: at displacement nodes they stay still and the pressure varies most; at displacement antinodes they move most and the pressure hardly changes. An open end is a displacement antinode (a pressure node); a closed end is a displacement node (a pressure antinode).

both open or both closed: `f_n = n v/(2L)`, `n = 1, 2, 3, ...`;   open and closed: `f_n = n v/(4L)`, `n = 1, 3, 5, ...`

The molecule view, the rainbow colouring and the traces show how the air moves in each harmonic.
