In this simulation, you can explore the Coulomb force between point charges. The simulation shows three charges. A positive charge is indicated with a plus sign (+), if its corresponding slider is set to a positive value, and a negative charge with a minus sign (−), if its slider is set to a negative value. You can drag the charges around the screen, or use the sliders, to change their positions. The buttons display or remove an arrow representing the net Coulomb force on each charge.

The force between two point charges `q_1` and `q_2` a distance `r` apart is given by Coulomb's law:

`F = k (|q_1 q_2|)/r^2`,   `k = 8.99 xx 10^9 " N m"^2"/C"^2`.

Like charges repel and opposite charges attract. The net force on each charge is the vector sum of the forces from the other two:

`vec F_1 = vec F_(12) + vec F_(13)`.

Doubling a charge doubles every force on it; doubling a distance divides the force by four.
