If the loop sits at a dead spot (θ = 0° or 180°), press Nudge to give it a starting kick. Reverse the field (φ = 180°) and the motor spins the other way. Turn it to 90° and see what happens when the commutator switches at the wrong moment.

Force on each wire F = I L × B (perpendicular to both the wire and B; |F| = I L B)  |  I = U / R  |  Torque τ = I A B sin(θ − φ), loop area A = 0.010 m²  |  The brushes reverse the loop current whenever sin θ changes sign; that keeps the torque in one sense only when B is horizontal (φ = 0° or 180°)  |  Moment of inertia · dω/dt = τ − bω
