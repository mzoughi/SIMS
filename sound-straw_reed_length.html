Straw Reed — Cut It Shorter

Length: 11.0 cm | Frequency ≈ 780 Hz | Note ≈ G5 | λ ≈ 44.0 cm
Straw length (like cutting it with scissors)
L = 11.0 cm
The reed end (left) acts closed; the open end (right) is open — a quarter-wavelength pipe, just like the cut-straw demo.

A drinking straw with its end pinched flat into a reed: blowing makes the reed buzz, and the air column in the straw picks the note. The reed end acts as a closed end and the far end is open, so the straw holds a quarter wavelength:

`lambda = 4L`,   `f = v/(4L)`.

Cutting the straw shorter, as with scissors, raises the note: half the length, twice the frequency, an octave higher.
