A spring launches a wheeled block up a frictionless incline. Along the incline, the only force is the component of gravity `m g sin theta`, so the size of the acceleration is `a = g sin theta`, the same going up and coming down.

Measure the acceleration at several angles and plot its size against `sin theta`: the points fall on a straight line through the origin, and its slope is `g`. This is a way to measure the acceleration due to gravity with a track that is only slightly tilted.
