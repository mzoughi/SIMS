What's happening

A target graph is loaded. Press Start Attempt, then drag the cart to try to match it.

A target position-time graph is shown, but not the motion behind it. Drag the cart along the 1.2 m track during an attempt to recreate the graph: your attempt is drawn on top of the target as you go, and the feedback tells you how well they match. Try again as often as you like.

Where the graph is steep, the cart must move fast; where it is flat, the cart is at rest; where it slopes down, the cart moves back toward the start. The slope of the graph is the velocity:

`v = (Delta x)/(Delta t)`.
