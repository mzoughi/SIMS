Plane water waves meet a boundary between two depths of water, where they travel at different speeds. Part of the wave is reflected, at the same angle as it arrived, and part is transmitted, bending toward or away from the boundary. The frequency is the same on both sides, so the wavelength changes with the speed:

`(sin theta_i)/v_1 = (sin theta_t)/v_2`,   `lambda = v/f`,

`R = (v_2 - v_1)/(v_1 + v_2)`,   `T = (2 v_2)/(v_1 + v_2)`,

where `R` and `T` are the reflected and transmitted amplitudes as fractions of the incident one. The ray diagram shows the three rays; drag the boundary and the ray marker on the tank.
