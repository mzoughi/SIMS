Results

A position-time graph describes a cart on a frictionless track. Read its position at each listed time, then calculate the average velocity over each interval and the average acceleration between successive velocities:

`bar v = (Delta x)/(Delta t)`,   `bar a = (Delta v)/(Delta t)`.

The cart's true motion stays hidden: only the graph is real data. After you submit, the sim grades your entries, graphs your values against the true ones, and plays the motion back on the 1.2 m track so you can check your work.
