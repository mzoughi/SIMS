Standing Waves in a Flute

Note:
Show: Harmonic:

A flute's air column is open at both ends (the embouchure hole acts as an open end), so it behaves as an open-open pipe: all harmonics fit, and the lowest note has a wavelength twice the column's length.

`f_n = n v/(2 L_"eff")`,   `n = 1, 2, 3, 4`.

Opening finger holes shortens the effective length and raises the note. Blowing harder (overblowing) jumps to the second harmonic, an octave higher. The displacement or pressure of the standing wave and the motion of the air molecules can be shown, slowed down if needed.
